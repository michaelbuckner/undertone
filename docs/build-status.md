**v0.1.1 timing and recall changes.** MIDI events now retain their input timestamps and render at offsets within audio buffers. Onsets follow actual MIDI ticks rather than extrapolated tempo, including during 120→90→140 BPM changes. The timeline buffers two nominal audio blocks, tracks slow audio/wall-clock drift, rejects stale/invalid/previous-source events, and requires a fresh Start after overflow or a major callback discontinuity. Late-event accounting tolerates one sample of boundary correction; it does not silently discard larger late deliveries.

The deterministic timestamp matrix measured a maximum rounding error of 0.006803 ms. A twenty-minute simulation with 80 ppm audio-clock drift, ±0.8 ms callback scheduling jitter, and 0.5 ms MIDI delivery latency measured 0.0971 ms p95 and 0.1011 ms maximum onset error after fixed-delay compensation, with no late events or stream resets. These numbers describe the implemented simulation, not a Windows/macOS driver or Elektron measurement. New regressions also cover Evolve loading/starting the saved realized phrase rather than prematurely applying its next variation.

**Implementation limits to retain in review.** MIDI buffering adds a fixed two-block delay in addition to device output latency; reducing the audio buffer reduces that delay. OS delivery delays larger than the available lookback can still produce late events, which are counted in the UI. External MIDI note output, DAW plug-ins, arbitrary scene chains and footswitch mapping remain unimplemented. The v0.8 revision adds generated MIDI export, live WAV capture and A/B alternation. Generative decisions run in bounded, allocation-free C++ code at musical boundaries. Synth/effect algorithms are intentionally compact and need subjective sound refinement. No hardware code or purchases have been made.
