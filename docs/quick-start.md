**Playing the Undertone prototype**

Open `Undertone.exe` from the Windows package. Start with your monitoring volume low, as you would with a new instrument. The app opens stopped; it never starts sounding automatically.

Version 0.11 opens in the compact **Performance** view. **Sound** now fits the same height, with Tone, Envelope, Effects, Layers, Percussion, and Phrase & MIDI sections. Your last section stays selected when you return from Performance. See the [focused editor guide](sound-editor-0.10.md). The [performance guide](performance-0.8.md) covers A/B scenes, Hold/Vary/New motifs, intentional chord swaps, Give me space, snapshots, WAV recording and MIDI export. Percussion level now reaches 2.0; 1.0 is the previous maximum.

**Library** searches factory presets, saved sessions, kept moments and recent files. Selecting shows details; Load selected restores that item stopped. The header shows the current name and saved/edited state. Save updates the current session file; Library > Save variation creates a separately named copy. Loading over edits offers Save copy & load, Discard & load, or Cancel. See the [library guide](library-0.11.md).

The app starts with **Studies / Quiet room**. Try it for a sparse, restrained piece, or choose **Studies / Fault line** for darker tension. Let each run for roughly two minutes to hear its full arc. **Composition**, on Performance or in Sound > Phrase & MIDI, selects Song, Still or Uneasy generation. The latter two use open/tension fields and sparse gestures; Let layers come and go enables their 32-bar form. **Surface** mixes a quiet friction-like texture. **Melody voice** now also includes Felt object and Bowed wire. See [composition and texture](composition-0.6.md).

1. Open Audio settings and select the output connected to your speakers or headphones. The first local test detected the MOTU M Series; your selected route may differ. Start with a 256- or 512-sample buffer if supported. The app remembers its audio settings.
2. Choose a starting character. Ambient offers long pads; neo-soul adds richer voicings; funk uses short syncopated stabs; dark ambient offers slow minor harmony and evolving texture. Selecting a character loads its settings and stops playback explicitly. Pad tone changes the sound independently, keeping your phrase and tempo.
3. Choose Key and Mode, then press Generate. Press Play / restart to loop. Space toggles playback; Escape triggers Panic. Stop releases notes naturally; Panic also clears effect tails.
4. Adjust Lowest note in Sound > Phrase & MIDI and Bass cut in Sound > Effects to leave room for your bass. Lowest note is an absolute MIDI note floor, displayed with its note name. Bass cut removes low audio frequencies independently. Omit root leaves chord roots to the bassist; locked chords retain their existing voicings.
5. Click a chord card to lock it, or Lock phrase to keep everything. Unlock before changing key, register, spacing, or phrase length. A lock made during an incompatible queued edit cancels that edit and reports it.
6. Enable Evolve, then choose its target and interval in Sound > Phrase & MIDI. Chords + tone changes unlocked chords at the selected bar interval while slowly morphing brightness, texture and space. Chords only and Tone only are also available. Positive Variation guarantees at least one unlocked chord changes at each automatic harmonic boundary; higher values change more chords and deepen tone movement. Zero pauses both targets. Lock the whole phrase to keep the progression while tone evolves. The chord strip shows a countdown, current tone values and pending chord names.
7. Save a named `.undertone` session. Open restores its realized phrase and settings, stopped at the phrase beginning. Undo reverses one harmonic change; it does not undo live sound-macro adjustments.

For more movement around the chords, try **Memory / Sun-faded** or **Memory / Afterimage** under Starting Character. Open Sound > Layers to mix Pad bed, Broken chords and Melody independently. Drift / wear softens and slightly destabilizes the tone; Figure echo supplies repeats. Let layers come and go alternates the detail parts across sixteen bars. New motif changes the recurring melodic preferences while retaining your harmony. These are optional additions; older presets keep them disabled. Choose **Melody voice** for Original / Hollow, Soft sine, Felt keys or Air flute. The voice changes on new notes and keeps the backing and melody choices intact. The melody now looks ahead across upcoming chords and controls incompatible ringing notes. See [melody phrasing and voices](melody-0.5.md).

Use **Swap** under a chord to replace just that slot, or **Rest / Restore** to silence it and bring back its stored voicing. Locked chords protect both actions. These edits take effect immediately when stopped, or on the next bar while playing. If a whole-phrase edit is already queued, they join it at its existing loop boundary. Other harmonic edits take effect at the next complete phrase boundary and take priority over automatic changes. Evolve controls respond immediately and use their own interval. Brightness, Motion, Space, Level, and effects respond immediately with smoothing. Play / restart begins from the first chord and applies pending manual edits.

Under **Automation** on Performance, enable **Alternate Still / Uneasy** and/or **Cycle motif**, each with its own 1–64-bar interval. These are stepped, tempo-synced controls; they follow internal or MIDI transport. Composition alternation keeps chord roots, locked notes, rests and sounds; it pauses in Song mode. Try composition every 16 bars and motifs every 8 bars. See [chord edits and cycles](cycles-0.7.md) for timing, priority and recall behavior.

For a light rhythmic backing, enable **Percussion** beside the upper performance controls. Open Sound > Percussion for its Level, Density, Glitch, Color and New pattern controls. Density adds offbeats; Glitch adds brief bursts around a stable quarter-note pulse. New pattern keeps your chords and melody. Turn percussion off when your Elektron supplies the rhythm. See [percussion details](percussion-0.4.md).

The current phrase is what Save and recovery preserve. A manually queued, not-yet-playing edit is not included; apply it first if you want to retain it. Seed, motif state, melody voice, composition mode, Surface, percussion settings, rests, cycle controls and tone-cycle phase are saved; playback restarts at the phrase beginning rather than restoring an exact performance position or effect tails. Version 0.11 still saves schema 8. Schemas 1–7 import with new statement/answer phrasing disabled and no scene bank; existing percussion levels are unchanged. Earlier schemas retain their documented defaults for composition, voices, rests and cycles. New schema 8 saves cannot be opened by earlier app versions; retain originals if you need to return to them. Unsupported or invalid files are rejected without changing the current session.

**Using an Elektron clock.** Connect one clock leader to the computer by USB MIDI, or connect its MIDI Out to the MOTU/interface MIDI In. In Sound > Phrase & MIDI, refresh the MIDI list and choose that input under Clock source. Enable MIDI clock and transport send on the chosen Elektron device, with port routing appropriate to your connection. Then press Play on the Elektron. Incoming Start begins the phrase, Stop releases it, and Continue resumes it. After a clock loss or source change, send a fresh Start. Device-specific menu steps depend on model and installed firmware; consult the manuals linked in the software PRD.

This build receives clock and transport only. It does not send generated MIDI notes, clock, program changes, or audio to your Elektron over that MIDI connection. Mix the app's audio output with your drums and bass through your existing audio setup. Physical sync with Digitone II, Digitakt I, and Syntakt still needs validation; input-port detection is not proof of timing quality.

Incoming timestamps are preserved and mapped within each audio block. The MIDI buffer uses two audio blocks of lookback, which adds a fixed delay plus the output device's own latency. The bottom timing label shows that buffer delay and the late-event count. At 48 kHz with 256-sample buffers, the MIDI buffer adds about 10.7 ms; at 96 kHz with 1024 samples it adds about 21.3 ms. A smaller audio buffer shortens this delay if your computer/interface can run it reliably. These are calculated buffering delays, not measured end-to-end audio latency. A timing-queue overflow or major audio callback gap stops external playback and requires a fresh Start.

**Recovery and device issues.** The Recover button offers the last automatic session copy. Once you interact with a session, the app updates that copy roughly every ten seconds and on normal exit; opening the app alone does not replace an existing recovery. Saves retain a `.bak` of the prior file. The audio settings and recovery live under `%APPDATA%/Undertone` on Windows and the platform application-data location on macOS. Tests can redirect that directory with `UNDERTONE_DATA_DIR`.

If no audio device is active, open Audio settings to select one. If you lose external clock, the app stops scheduling notes after 500 ms, releases existing notes, and displays Clock Lost. Reconnect and restart the leader. MIDI input errors and control conflicts are shown in the bottom status line. The window scrolls on smaller displays so controls remain reachable.

**First jam to try.** Choose Neo-soul / Sunday, D Dorian, four chords, one bar per chord, and about 88 BPM. Generate until a phrase suggests a bass line, then lock it. Try Omit root before locking if you want to supply all the roots yourself. A useful next feedback pass is whether this stays enjoyable for twenty minutes, and which controls you reach for while holding the bass.


**Interface size (v0.12).** Auto chooses 100-200% from the usable display area, after Windows/macOS display scaling. The footer shows the effective scale. Choose a fixed percentage there if desired; it is remembered independently of sessions. Small windows scroll rather than shrinking the controls. Auto follows the window to another display.

**Refreshed starting points.** Ambient and Dark ambient now use sparse Still/Uneasy forms. Neo-soul and Memory use Lyrical phrasing; Funk uses Pocket. In Sound > Layers, Phrase style changes the Song arrangement: Classic keeps the previous behavior, Lyrical uses lower, spaced phrases, and Pocket uses short offbeat gestures. Enable Statement / answer phrasing on Performance for the new five-note phrase patterns. Let layers come and go enables the form. Studies remains unchanged. Existing saved sessions keep their realized settings and default to Classic when loaded from older formats.
