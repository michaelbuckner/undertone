**Undertone 0.3 — parts that relate to each other**

Mike's feedback was that the evolving chords still felt bland, with Boards of Canada as the new reference. This pass adds musical structure around the harmony: a repeating tune, a rhythmically independent broken-chord figure, gaps, answering phrases and gentle degradation. The melodies and synthesis are original; no recordings or melodies by Boards of Canada are used.

The design reference is the emphasis on melodic development and recording texture in their [Exclaim interview](https://exclaim.ca/music/article/boards_of_canadas_seasons_in_sun), including their account of developing ideas by playing melodies to tape. The [Pitchfork interview](https://pitchfork.com/features/interview/6151-boards-of-canada/) also describes early experiments with successive tape recordings. The choices below are our interpretation for this instrument, not a claim to reproduce their studio techniques or sound exactly.

**The musical roles.** Pad bed is the existing synth voice, with its own mix control. Broken chords adds a woody, short-decay figure that chooses notes from the currently sounding chord. Melody adds a softer, hollow upper part following the selected scale. Its opening contour repeats every four bars; the second four-bar phrase changes the ending and resolves to a nearby chord tone. New motif selects a different contour while retaining the harmony, tempo and locks. The current bank contains four original melodic contours and two rhythmic figures, with seeded velocity variation. It is a bounded musical vocabulary, not an unconstrained composition model.

Let layers come and go enables a repeating sixteen-bar form: eight bars with both detail parts, four with the broken-chord figure absent, then four with the melody absent. Existing notes decay naturally at those transitions. The bed remains available throughout. Switch it off for a consistent accompaniment texture. None of these changes generate drums or a bass line.

Drift / wear adds small slow and fast pitch deviations plus softened, gently saturated high frequencies. Figure echo adds a filtered stereo cross-feedback delay to the detail parts, at three quarters of a beat. Delay-time changes are smoothed when tempo changes. This is compact synthetic coloration, not sampled tape noise, a cassette circuit model or a field-recording layer. The source sounds remain fully generated by the engine.

**Controls and recall.** The Arrangement controls sit below Evolve settings and are reachable by scrolling. They respond live with smoothing. Starting Character now includes Memory / Sun-faded (F major, 82 BPM) and Memory / Afterimage (D minor, 76 BPM). Both use two bars per chord, slow tone-only evolution and the optional sixteen-bar arrangement. Tone Patch still changes the underlying synth without changing chords or detail settings. Changing Starting Character replaces the complete setup and stops playback.

All new notes are bounded by the selected note floor and MIDI 108, follow the selected scale, and honor root omission. Chord locks freeze the harmony rather than silencing the related melody. The figure timing uses eighth-note positions on the internal audio timeline or every twelfth incoming MIDI clock tick. It introduces no timing randomness or swing in this version. Ordinary Stop releases the detail notes and lets effects decay; Panic clears the added voices and echo buffers as well as the original engine.

Schema 3 saves the layer mix, wear, echo, arrangement switch and motif seed. Schema 1 and 2 imports leave the additions disabled and the bed at its original level. Version 0.2 cannot read schema 3 saves. Save preserves musical settings and the realized chord phrase; loading restarts at the beginning, not at an exact past effect-tail state. The twelve extra detail voices are separate from the sixteen bed voices; hardware feasibility must account for both.

**Auditions for the phone.** `dist/Undertone-0.3.0-audio/` contains Sun-faded and Afterimage (48 seconds of playing plus four seconds of release), and a shorter plain-chord comparison from the same Sun-faded harmony/tone. The previews use the app's actual engine. Offline preview gain targets RMS 0.10 with peak headroom capped at 0.85, followed by a short fade; the preview does not add instruments or production effects. This gain is for comfortable auditioning and does not change the app's saved output level. WAV masters are 48 kHz stereo; MP3 copies are 192 kbps for easier phone playback. No bass or drums have been added, leaving those roles to Mike and the Elektron boxes.

**Verification.** Tests cover deterministic event selection, repeated motif identity, answering-form rests, all twelve roots/four modes at three register floors, root omission, new-motif changes, actual extra note events, finite audio at 44.1/48/96 kHz, live detail changes retaining harmony, Stop/Panic, exact MIDI-grid event counts, schema 1/2 migration and schema 3 roundtrips. The original four ambient/neo-soul six-second WAV references remain byte-identical with the additions disabled. Native UI layout is checked using a fresh smoke capture. Musical appeal still needs Mike's listening response; Mac execution and physical Elektron synchronization are not established by these checks.

Useful future directions after this audition: record your own tiny environmental sounds and transform them; add editable motif notes and rhythm; introduce carefully chosen modal borrowing; or let a motif move between a foreground voice and a quieter echo. Those are future options, not implemented features in this build.
